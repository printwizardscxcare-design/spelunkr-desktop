This update makes the channel keys work everywhere and improves Whisper captions.

- **Up / Down changes channels in windowed and fullscreen views.** Typing, sliders, and open dialogs keep their normal keyboard controls.
- **Captions start off.** Choose **Captions → Caption engine → None** to turn them off explicitly.
- **Turning captions on rewinds 15 seconds once.** The shortcut and the engine menu both do this. Changing recognition speed or channels doesn't add another rewind.
- **Faster Whisper updates.** The tiny model uses shorter audio batches and a smaller processing window. An overloaded computer skips an old waiting batch instead of building a long delay.
- **Pauses clear the screen.** Old queued blocks are discarded after reading time. Music or noise misidentified as speech can't keep old text visible indefinitely, and new dialogue starts a fresh block.

The transparent background, white/yellow text, five sizes, and two-line subtitle blocks remain available.
