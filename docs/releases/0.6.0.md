Whisper captions now come with the app on Windows, Mac, and Linux. This release also adds native Mac downloads for Intel processors and Apple M-series chips.

## What's new

- **Windows 10 captions.** Whisper works on Windows 10 and 11. Windows Live Captions is still an optional Windows 11 engine under **Captions → Caption engine**.
- **Mac apps.** Choose the Intel (`x64`) or Apple Silicon (`arm64`) DMG. Both require macOS 14.2 or newer. Open the DMG and drag the app into Applications.
- **The same speech engine on Linux.** Debian, Arch, and Fedora now use Whisper in place of Vosk.
- **Two included English models.** Faster is the default. Balanced uses the larger model if your computer can keep up. No speech account, subscription, Python install, or separate model download is needed.
- **Speech-based pause handling.** Captions stay visible while dialogue continues and clear after pauses, with time to read. Changing channels discards the earlier channel's pending audio.

Caption appearance is unchanged: transparent background, white or yellow text, five sizes, and blocks of up to two lines. Fullscreen captions adjust to the picture.

## Install and use

Choose an installer from the files below. GitHub's **Source code** archives are for building the project, not installing it.

[Windows setup](https://github.com/printwizardscxcare-design/spelunkr-desktop/blob/main/docs/WINDOWS.md) · [Mac setup and audio permission](https://github.com/printwizardscxcare-design/spelunkr-desktop/blob/main/docs/MAC.md) · [Linux setup](https://github.com/printwizardscxcare-design/spelunkr-desktop/blob/main/docs/LINUX.md)

Toggle captions with **Ctrl+Shift+L** on Windows/Linux or **Command+Shift+L** on Mac. Fullscreen is **F11** on Windows/Linux and **Control+Command+F** on Mac. Use **Up / Down** to change channels in fullscreen.

Recognition runs locally and uses speaker output, including other apps. The app doesn't select a microphone or save audio or transcripts. Recognition has a delay and can make mistakes.

## Preview notes

Windows installers are unsigned. Mac apps are signed locally but aren't notarized by Apple; the Mac guide covers the first launch. **Mac live speaker capture and its permission flow still need a physical-Mac test.** The [testing notes](https://github.com/printwizardscxcare-design/spelunkr-desktop/blob/main/docs/TESTING.md) explain what the automated checks cover, including Windows 10 and Linux desktop limits.

Checksums are included for each platform. The repository remains private, so downloads require an account with access. The [download-count report](https://github.com/printwizardscxcare-design/spelunkr-desktop/blob/main/docs/DOWNLOADS.md) now includes Mac DMGs.
