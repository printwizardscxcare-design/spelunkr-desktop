This is the first 0.8 beta. It adds a little breathing room for channel surfing: subtitles wait until you've settled on a channel.

## Subtitle cooldown

With captions on, changing channels clears the old text and waits for tuning to finish. Then a **five-second countdown** appears near the bottom-right of the video. Change channels again and the wait starts over for the new channel.

When the countdown ends, Whisper prepares its usual 15-second subtitle buffer. The cooldown and the caption buffer serve different purposes: the cooldown lets you browse; the buffer keeps subtitles lined up with the picture.

**Captions → Subtitle cooldown** lets you choose any whole second up to **30 seconds**, or **Off**. It defaults to **5 seconds**, remembers your choice, and works in fullscreen too. Pausing or seeking within the same channel doesn't start the countdown.

## Download and install

This beta also fixes an intermittent speech-detection startup problem that could leave captions blank on some machines.

Installers for Windows, Intel and Apple Silicon Macs, Debian, Arch, and Fedora are attached below. Both English Whisper models are included.

- **Windows 10/11, 64-bit:** Open the Setup.exe and follow the installer.
- **Mac, macOS 14.2+:** Choose arm64 for Apple Silicon or x64 for Intel. Open the DMG and drag the app into Applications.
- **Debian, Arch, or Fedora, 64-bit:** Use the matching package and your usual package manager. See [Linux setup](https://github.com/printwizardscxcare-design/spelunkr-desktop/blob/main/docs/LINUX.md), substituting the beta package filename in the commands.

The beta installs over an existing copy of the app. You can return to 0.7.0 by quitting and reinstalling it from the [current release](https://github.com/printwizardscxcare-design/spelunkr-desktop/releases/tag/v0.7.0). Preferences are kept. There is no automatic updater.

## Still a beta

This is an unofficial app, and generated captions can get words or timing wrong. Windows installers are unsigned, and Mac builds aren't notarized by Apple. Automated checks cover all platforms, but physical Mac viewing, gamepads, and the full range of Linux desktops still need testing.

Both the current release and this beta will remain available. Further 0.8 changes will stay in the beta until a regular release is requested.
