# Spelunkr Desktop 0.8.0-beta.2

Open the app, choose a channel, and captions prepare automatically. Built-in Whisper and the English models are included. No speech account, microphone setup, or separate model download is needed.

- Captions default to On with Whisper, including upgrades from older versions. On/Off is separate from engine choice; None was removed from the engine menu. Later choices are remembered.
- Subtitle connections retry automatically after an interruption.
- Audio processing now fades abrupt starts and stops, removes recording DC offsets, and resets gain smoothly.
- Normalization keeps ordinary programs at a more comfortable level; brief peaks are handled by the output limiter.
- Channel surfing keeps the five-second cooldown after tuning.

This beta replaces the previous downloads. Quit the app before installing it. Windows, Intel/Apple Silicon Mac, Debian, Arch, and Fedora installers include the local caption runtime.

Windows builds are unsigned and Mac builds are ad-hoc signed; operating-system first-launch prompts may appear. Recognition is English and wording or timing can be imperfect. Watching needs internet access.
